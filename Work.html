Selected research and engineering work spanning trustworthy AI, recommender systems, multimodal learning, software, embedded systems and applied data science.

01
LLMsPythonEvaluation

Benchmarking LLM trustworthiness

McGill University · ECE · 2026 · Supervisor: Prof. Di Wu

Designed repeated model-evaluation experiments for domain-specific question answering. Built Python pipelines for batch evaluation, metric logging and consistency analysis, then investigated category-level failures and variance across repeated runs.

  • Compared multiple language models under a consistent evaluation setup.
  • Analyzed category accuracy, invalid responses, consistency and variability.
  • Produced reproducible plots and an IEEE-format technical research report.
02
PyTorchRecommenders

Denoising implicit feedback

Dhirubhai Ambani University · 2023 — 2025

Developed and evaluated a label-denoising approach for implicit-feedback recommendation on multi-dataset experiments containing millions of interactions.

  • Built reproducible preprocessing, training and evaluation workflows.
  • Evaluated ranking quality with Recall@N and NDCG@N.
  • Conducted hyperparameter, ablation and datapoint-level analyses.
03
MultimodalNLPCV

Multimodal hateful-meme detection

University of Hildesheim · Germany · Jul — Aug 2024

Investigated multimodal hate-speech detection across four research datasets using LLM-based and multimodal approaches.

  • Worked across literature review, preprocessing, model benchmarking and error analysis.
  • Evaluated systems using accuracy, F1, recall and AUC.
  • Worked in a research area that also informs my broader interest in multilingual and multimodal harmful-content detection.

MLEnergyData

Machine learning for renewable energy

Smart Energy Learning Centre · Dhirubhai Ambani University · 2024 — 2025

Prepared and assessed research datasets for renewable-energy applications, developed predictive/prescriptive analyses and explored NLP/foundation-model approaches for related research problems.
